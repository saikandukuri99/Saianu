# Solix Empower 2026 AV: leadership feedback analysis (v11 → v12)

## Feedback received
1. "It looks AI generated." They want a human touch; the AV should look human-created.
2. Make it human, appealing, engaging and convincing.
3. Audience: customer CXOs, CIOs, CMOs, COOs, CEOs, IT heads, leadership and industry-specific leaders.
4. Engaging from the first second to the last.
5. Deliver at 1080p for review.

## What v11 is (3:18, 1920x1080, 30 fps)
Entirely Python-rendered motion graphics, a synthetic TTS voice (Kokoro), procedural music and screenshots in browser frames. No people, no real footage, no real voice.

## Why it reads as "AI generated"
| Signal in v11 | Why viewers read it as machine-made |
|---|---|
| No humans anywhere | Every frame is UI, icons or abstract shapes. There is no face, hand or workplace. |
| Synthetic voice-over (Kokoro af_heart) | Even with EQ, the pacing is uniform and the breath, emphasis and emotion are missing. It is the biggest tell. |
| Procedural music | Loops with no musical arc and no build or release at story beats. |
| Floating glossy spheres and bokeh particles | A stock "AI/tech" cliché that appears on every scene. |
| Generic glossy red bolt, 3D wheel, hologram | Template-like visuals that do not feel authored. |
| Same layout repeated | Title top-left, browser card, bullets on the right, pill caption. This repeats for about 2 minutes. |
| Product screenshots dominate (about 60% of runtime) | It reads as a UI tour, not a story. Low-resolution screenshots are softened and zoomed. |
| Word-by-word subtitle pill and dense on-screen text | It reads like a webinar and competes with the narration. |
| No emotional arc | Facts are listed ("Step One, Step Two") with no problem, tension or payoff for a CXO. |
| Script is feature-led | There is no customer pain point, outcome or proof story in the first 20 seconds. |

## Audience lens
- CEO/COO care about outcomes, risk and time to value.
- CIO/IT head care about trust, governance and integration.
- CMO cares about the story and brand confidence.
- Industry leaders (healthcare, pharma) care about relevance to their sector.

v11 speaks to features. v12 should speak to the stakes: "your data is your advantage, and AI is only as good as the data under it".

## v12 creative direction
**Principle: a human leads, the product supports.**
1. **Cold open (0:00–0:15):** real-feeling human moments: a CIO in a late-night boardroom, a clinician at a screen, a plant floor. One line of tension ("Everyone has an AI strategy. Few have the data to back it."), then the Solix logo.
2. **Human voice-over:** a real recorded narrator (or a premium cloned/licensed voice with the speaker's consent), with natural pacing, pauses and emphasis. Music that builds at the beats.
3. **Fewer, bigger ideas:** cut the browser-card tour to the 3–4 hero moments (AI-Ready, AI-Activated, Move It Once, EAI Healthcare/Pharma). Show the real UI as short, sharp inserts, not full-scene screens.
4. **Hand-crafted motion language:** kinetic typography with real easing and overshoot, camera-style moves, shallow depth of field and film grain. Remove spheres and bokeh and use one consistent colour grade.
5. **Proof with faces:** customer-logo moment plus analyst recognition (Gartner/IDC/Forrester/Bloor) as a confident, quick beat. A customer quote on camera if one is available.
6. **Close:** a human line ("Let's build the agentic enterprise together") and the Empower 2026 lockup.
7. **Pacing:** a visual change every 2–4 seconds, one idea per scene, and a rising energy curve. No dead air.
