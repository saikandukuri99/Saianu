# Solix EAI Film v14 (Pune): network background version

- `Solix_EAI_Film_v14_network_bg.mp4`: the approved v14 film (100.6 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 High) with the plexus network video layered into the background. The voice-over and music are the original final mix, copied bit for bit, so timing and loudness are unchanged.
- `Solix_EAI_Film_v14_captions.srt`: the captions, unchanged (no timing moved).
- `preview_frames.jpg`: frames at 1.5 s, 20 s, 45 s and 98 s.

## How the background is applied
- The 20 s plexus clip is converted to 30 fps and ping-ponged (forward, then reversed) to cover 100.6 s with no jump cut.
- It is screen-blended in RGB over the film, so it only adds light. White text, the logo and the QR code are not affected.
- Opacity is 100% from 0 to 0.5 s (music-only opening), fades to 20% by 2.5 s, and stays at 20% to the end.
- The picture content, the timings and the four pending storyboard edits are left exactly as in the reference.
