# Reel B-roll – live-action shot list (9:16, 1080×1920, 5–7 s each)

Look of the reference clips: dim server room or office, cool blue light, LED racks blurred into bokeh, slow handheld or dolly moves, people working on screens, tablets or holographic panels.
Grade every clip to the Solix palette: crush the blacks to navy (#061232), push the highlights toward brand blue (#0088cf), and use red (#ee2424) only as accents (status LEDs, rim light, UI dots).
Plain clips only: no on-screen text.

For each clip there are **stock search terms** (Envato Elements, Adobe Stock, Storyblocks, Pexels) and an **AI-video prompt** you can paste into Veo, Sora, Runway, Kling or Firefly Video.

---

## 1. The Evolution of AI
- **Search:** `vintage computer CRT screen close up dark`, `retro computer to futuristic AI transition`, `old terminal green text`, `AI chip hologram server room vertical`
- **Prompt:** A photorealistic vertical 9:16 shot, 6 seconds. A close-up of a 1980s beige CRT computer on a desk in a dark room, with blue light glowing from the screen. The camera slowly pushes in toward the screen. As it reaches the screen, the frame dissolves into a modern dark-blue server room, where a glowing holographic AI microchip floats above a desk, surrounded by fine light particles. The lighting is deep navy with cool blue highlights and small red status LEDs. Cinematic, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, no text, no logos.
